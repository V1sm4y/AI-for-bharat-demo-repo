# AI SkillFit: OCR & Integrity Requirements

This document outlines the technical and functional requirements for the OCR (Identity Verification) and Proctoring (Anti-Cheat) modules of the AI SkillFit platform.

---

## 1. OCR & Identity Verification Requirements

The system must verify the candidate's identity before starting the interview.

### Functional Requirements:
- **Aadhaar Card OCR**: Extract Name, Date of Birth, and Gender from the front of the Aadhaar card.
- **Voter ID/Driving License**: Support fallback for other government-issued IDs.
- **Data Validation**: Cross-reference the extracted OCR data with the profile data provided during onboarding.
- **Blur Detection**: Reject images with low sharpness to ensure high-accuracy OCR results.
- **Glare Detection**: Detect and prompt the user to retake photos if lighting makes the card unreadable.

### Technical Dependencies:
- **Frontend**: `tesseract.js` for on-device OCR (speed) or Google Cloud Vision API (accuracy).
- **Backend**: `pytesseract` or `easyocr` for server-side processing of high-res uploads.
- **Preprocessing**: `canvas` (web) or `opencv-python` (backend) for grayscale conversion and thresholding.

---

## 2. Face Verification Requirements

Continuous matching of the live candidate against a reference photo.

### Functional Requirements:
- **Reference Capture**: Capture a high-quality "Anchor Selfie" at the start of the session.
- **Match Confidence**: Maintain a confidence score > 75% for "Clear" status.
- **Face Count**: Detect and flag if multiple faces appear in the frame.
- **Liveness Detection**: Detect simple spoofing (e.g., holding a photo to the camera) using head-pose variation.

### Technical Dependencies:
- `face-api.js`: Used for face detection, landmarking, and descriptor matching on the web.
- `expo-face-detector`: Native face tracking for mobile clients.
- `tensorflow.js`: Backend for advanced liveness models.

---

## 3. Proctoring & Anti-Cheat (The "Other Stuff")

Monitoring candidate behavior during the voice interview with Priya.

### Monitoring Points:
- **Visibility Concerns**: Flag if the face is not visible for more than 5 seconds.
- **Head Turns**: Detect significant head rotation (looking away from the screen for answers).
- **App Switching**: (Web) Detect when the user switches tabs or minimizes the browser.
- **Browser Integrity**: Detect if Developer Tools are opened during the assessment.

### Severity Levels:
- **Warning**: Minor violation (e.g., face obscured briefly). Triggers a subtle UI alert.
- **Critical**: Major violation (e.g., left the app). Triggers an immediate session block.
- **Automatic Block**: Session terminates after 3 warnings or 1 critical violation.

---

## 4. Audio Integrity Requirements

Ensuring the voice interview is conducted by the candidate alone.

### Requirements:
- **Silence Detection**: Flag if the candidate is silent for more than 15 seconds during a question.
- **Background Noise**: Filter out common environmental noise while keeping the candidate's voice clear.
- **Speaker Diarization**: Detect if a second person is speaking or prompting the candidate.
